Engineering AI Agents: schedule and papers

Topics, dates, videos and slides follow the official course schedule. On top of that, every lecture gets a block of the papers cited in its slides: extracted from the PDFs and matched against arXiv by author, year and title.

151 papers in total. A paper cited by two lectures appears in both blocks, so the blocks do not add up to 151.
